Questions

FAQ

How are the ₹ figures worked out?

Each trade is 1 lot (65 qty); on strong KP signals the system adds one more lot (the add-on lot), which is included. Rupee values are estimated from the option move, after costs. Your own results depend on your broker, fills and costs.

How much margin do I need?

Minimum ₹25,000 per lot, and up to ₹1,00,000 per lot on peak days. Margin rules change, so confirm with your broker.

Do you give tips or signals?

No. This is a public record of an automated system for learning and transparency. Nothing on this site is investment advice.
